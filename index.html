About Me

I am a computational scientist working at the interface of molecular dynamics simulations, computational biophysics, DNA nanotechnology and machine learning.


My research focuses on understanding the structure, dynamics, thermodynamics and mechanical properties of biomolecular systems using molecular simulations and physics-based computational approaches.

Research
